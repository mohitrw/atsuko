PHONEME-BY-PHONEME BREAKDOWN:

   - Frequency: ~250 Hz - 2,500 Hz.
   - Acoustic Strength: Voiced nasal murmur gives good initial vocal cord voicing.
     Decent clarity from quiet-to-moderate distance.

2. Nucleus Vowel: /aɪ/ (Wide Diphthong: Open Front /a/ -> Near-Close /ɪ/)
   - Acoustic Signature: Large resonant formant shift (F1 drops from 850 Hz to
     400 Hz, F2 climbs from 1,200 Hz to 2,100 Hz).

3. Coda: /t/ (Voiceless Alveolar Plosive Stop)
   - Acoustic Signature: 40ms - 60ms silent occlusion followed by a transient burst
     at 3,000 Hz - 5,000 Hz.
   - Comparison with 'X' (/ks/):

     ending 'X' in "NX", "NOX", "NYX", or "ONYX". It does not cut through heavy bass
     or room echo as reliably.
