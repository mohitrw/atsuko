SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSION & FINAL RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================

Why did "Sure" feel so magical?
Because the English "SH" sound (/ʃ/) produces intense acoustic energy between
3,000 Hz and 7,000 Hz, which effortlessly penetrates room distance, ceiling
fans, and music bass.

However, you CANNOT use "Sure" because it is a common conversation word that
will falsely trigger every time anyone says "are you sure" or "for sure".

And you CANNOT use invented/foreign spellings (like Eimi, Nyxn, Scaaf, Eryx)
because Google's English Speech Recognizer will mangle them into random English
words (the exact reason Asuko only worked 3-4/10 times).
