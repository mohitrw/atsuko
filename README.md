# 🎵 Atsuko — Offline Music Player

Atsuko is a free, offline music player designed for people who want to enjoy their own music collection without streaming services or an internet connection.

Designed by **Mohit RW** and brought to life using Antigravity IDE, Atsuko combines original UI design with functional development to create a clean, minimal music-listening experience.

## ✨ Features

* **Offline Music Playback** — Play music directly from your local files without an internet connection.
* **Local Music Library** — Import and manage your own music collection.
* **Progressive Web App** — Install Atsuko on supported devices for app-like access.
* **Responsive Design** — Enjoy the interface on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* **Persistent Library** — Your music library and album artwork can remain available between sessions, depending on browser storage permissions.
* **Playback Controls** — Control playback and navigate your music.
* **Queue Management** — Manage the songs you want to listen to next.
* **Custom UI Design** — An original, minimal interface designed by me.

## 🎨 Design & Development

Atsuko began as an original UI design concept and evolved into a functional music player.

I designed the interface and used Antigravity IDE for AI-assisted development, bringing the design to life as a working application.

The project focuses on:

* Clear navigation and music controls.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit the [Atsuko website](YOUR-GITHUB-PAGES-URL).
2. Install Atsuko as a PWA using your browser's install option, if available.
3. Open the application and select or import your local music folder.
4. Choose a song and start listening.
5. Enjoy your music offline.

## 🔒 Privacy

Atsuko is designed to play music from your local device. Your personal music files are not uploaded to a streaming service as part of normal playback.
