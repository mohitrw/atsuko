ATSUKO - UNDERSTANDING OF YOUR IDEA & STEP-BY-STEP IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
================================================================================

1. OUR UNDERSTANDING OF YOUR IDEA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you open a brand new Atsuko (or clear the app) and have a backup folder 
on your computer that contains your "atsuko.atu" file and your "Recordings" folder:

You go to Settings -> click "Change Folder" -> and pick your backup folder.
Atsuko detects your "atsuko.atu" file and shows the "Welcome Back" popup with:
- "Reconnect Songs Now" (White button)
- "I'll Reconnect Later" (Text button)

Here is how each part must behave according to your instructions:


A. SCENARIO 1: USER CLICKS "I'LL RECONNECT LATER"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CURRENT BUG:
  Currently, if you click "I'll Reconnect Later", Atsuko shows all the old songs
  in the All Songs list. But because they are just metadata from the .atu file
  without the actual audio files attached, they cannot play when clicked.

- WHAT IT MUST DO INSTEAD:
  Do NOT show any unplayable fake songs in the song list!
  Keep the songs list completely clean:
  - Only show real recordings that are physically present inside the "Recordings" 
    folder on your disk.
  - If no recordings exist in the folder, the song list stays empty ("No Songs").
  - The previous playlist and song metadata from the .atu file stays safely stored 
    in memory in the background, waiting to be linked when you actually add your 
    audio files.


B. SCENARIO 2: USER CLICKS "RECONNECT SONGS NOW"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When you click "Reconnect Songs Now" in the Welcome popup:
  It immediately opens the "Add Music to Atsuko" popup (the same popup you get 
  when you click the "+" button in the top-left corner).
- In this popup, you have two choices:
  1. Pick single / multiple music files one by one.
  2. Pick an entire music folder from your disk.


C. REMOVE "RECONNECT SONGS" BUTTON FROM SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In Settings, under "Atsuko Data Folder", the "Reconnect Songs" button will be 
  completely deleted from both the HTML and the JavaScript.
- Under "Atsuko Data Folder", only "Change Folder" will remain.
- The "+" (Add Music) button on the main screen handles adding music and 
  reconnecting songs at any time.


D. HOW ADDING SONGS & RECONNECTING WORKS (VIA "+" BUTTON OR "RECONNECT SONGS NOW")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Whenever you choose songs (whether one-by-one or an entire folder):

1. Choose In-App Folder:
   After selecting the audio files from your disk, Atsuko asks you to name an 
   in-app folder (e.g. "16 Nov" or your own custom name) or choose an existing one.
   Once you enter the name and confirm, the songs are assigned to that folder.

2. Cross-Check with "atsuko.atu":
   Atsuko checks every added audio file against the stored data in your .atu file 
   (by file hash, file size, title, and ID3 tags):
   - MATCHED SONGS:
     If a song was previously in your .atu file, Atsuko recognizes it and 
     automatically restores the playlists that contained that song, with the 
     exact same playlist names and songs inside them. It also restores their 
     favorite status, play counts, and listening stats.
   - NEW SONGS:
     If you add new MP3 files that were not in your .atu file before, Atsuko adds 
     them normally into All Songs and into your newly chosen in-app folder without 
     interfering with your playlists.
   - PLAYABLE IMMEDIATELY:
     All added songs are immediately loaded with their real audio files and can 
     be played instantly.

3. In-App Folders Structure:
   - The newly created in-app folder holds the songs you just added.
   - The "Recordings" folder automatically holds all your voice recordings.
   - Any songs not assigned to a specific folder stay in "Unsorted".

2. STEP-BY-STEP IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
================================================================================

STEP 1: FIX THE "I'LL RECONNECT LATER" BEHAVIOR
- In "js/app.js" (inside handleRestoreReturningUser and initDataFolder):
  Remove the code that pushes missing placeholder tracks into "allTracks".
  Keep "allTracks" empty of fake songs.
  Only load the actual recordings found on disk in the "Recordings" folder.
  Store the .atu song metadata in "window.dataFolderService.originalAtuSongs" 
  in the background so it can be matched when files are added.

STEP 2: LINK "RECONNECT SONGS NOW" TO THE ADD MUSIC MODAL
- In "js/onboardingWizard.js":
  When "Reconnect Songs Now" is clicked, close the Welcome modal and immediately 
  call "openAddMusicModal()".
  This shows the exact "Add Music to Atsuko" interface with the two options 
  (pick music files or pick a folder).

STEP 3: REMOVE "RECONNECT SONGS" FROM SETTINGS
- In "index.html":
  Delete the "<button id='settingsReconnectSongsBtn'>🔄 Reconnect Songs</button>" 
  from the settings modal.
- In "js/app.js":
  Remove the click event listener and handler for "settingsReconnectSongsBtn".

STEP 4: ENSURE IN-APP FOLDER PROMPT ALWAYS COMES FIRST WHEN IMPORTING
- In "js/app.js" (inside handleSelectedAudioFiles):
  When files are selected (whether single files or a folder), open the 
  in-app folder name dialog ("Add Songs to Folder" with date suggestion).
  When confirmed, pass the chosen folder to "executeImportSongs".

STEP 5: CROSS-CHECK AND AUTO-RESTORE PLAYLISTS & FAVORITES
- In "js/app.js" (inside executeImportSongs):
  1. For each added file, match it against "originalAtuSongs" from the .atu file 
     using the 256KB SHA-256 fingerprint, filename, size, and ID3 title.
  2. If matched:
     Link the real audio blob to the stored song, restore its favorite status, 
     and assign it to the in-app folder.
  3. Rebuild the playlists:
     Check all playlists in .atu. For any playlist that had this song, update 
     its track list to include this live song and save it.
  4. If unmatched (new song):
     Import it as a new track into All Songs and the selected in-app folder.
  5. Sync the updated data immediately to the .atu file on disk.

STEP 6: SERVICE WORKER & VERSION REFRESH
- Bump cache version in "sw.js" and asset version in "index.html" so changes 
  take effect cleanly upon reload.
